Fynqua is a technology platform that helps retailers and distributors offer banking, payments and recharge from their own counter.

Your customers already ask for these services. We make it easy for you to say yes.

Our story

Every day, people walk into a neighbourhood shop and ask for more than the shelves can give: cash, a bill payment, a recharge. Most shops send them elsewhere and lose the business.

Fynqua started to change that. We give small retailers and distributors a single dashboard to offer financial services, without needing capital, licences or a technical team of their own.

We are building with partner banks and trusted API providers so that shopkeepers earn on every transaction and customers get a service they can rely on. [Founding story, year and parent company: CONFIRM WITH FOUNDER]
