Services you can offer

Tap a service to see what it does. Every service runs from the same dashboard and the same login.

Money Transfer

Send money to any bank account for your customers.

Customers who need to send money home or pay someone can do it at your shop. You enter the details, the customer confirms, and you earn on the transfer.

Best for: shops near migrant workers, students and families.

AEPS

Cash withdrawal and balance check with Aadhaar.

Customers use their Aadhaar and fingerprint to withdraw cash, check balance or see a mini statement. No debit card needed.

Best for: villages and towns where a bank branch or ATM is far away.

Bill Payments

Electricity, water, gas, broadband and more.

Collect monthly bills at your counter and give the customer an instant confirmation. Customers skip the queue, and you earn on each bill.

Best for: regular customers who come in every month.

Recharge

Mobile and DTH recharge in seconds.

Recharge prepaid mobile numbers and DTH connections from the dashboard. It is quick, repeat business that brings people back to your shop.

Best for: every shop, every day.

Micro ATM & mPOS

A portable bank point for cash and card payments.

Offer cash withdrawal, balance enquiry and card payments with a small portable device, even where branches are far.

Best for: shops that want to serve cash and card customers.

CMS (Cash Management)

Collect and deposit cash with clear records.

Help businesses deposit and track cash collections, with a record of every transaction in one place.

Best for: distributors and businesses that handle cash from many locations.

CIBIL Report

Let customers check their credit score.

Customers can see their credit score and credit history through you, a useful first step before they apply for a loan.

Best for: customers planning a loan or a new credit card.
