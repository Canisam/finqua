What working here looks like

A small team on a hard problem. You will see your work reach a shopkeeper’s phone, not sit in a slide deck.

Real problems

Payments that fail, refunds that stall, commissions that need to be fair. You fix what retailers actually complain about.

Close to the customer

Sales, support and tech sit close to the retailer’s voice. Feedback reaches the team fast.

Ownership

Clear responsibility for your area, and room to take on more as the platform grows.

Learning on the job

Fintech, payments and compliance are learned by doing, with experienced people around you.
