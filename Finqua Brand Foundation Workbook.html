SECTION 6

Brand Personality & Tone

TrustworthyBoldSmoothSimple

If Finqua were a person

Ek confident, samajhdar dukaandar jo tech mein bhi tez hai. Seedhi baat karta hai, hisaab ka pakka hai, aur kabhi dara ke nahi bechta.

Language & sample line

Hinglish. Shop owners ko apni lagti hai. Hindi baad mein add ho sakti hai.

"Aapki dukaan, ab ek banking point. Har transaction par kamai."

Hum aise bolenge

  • Simple, seedhi bhasha
  • Confident aur honest
  • Friendly aur helpful

Hum aise nahi bolenge

  • Heavy financial jargon
  • Over-promise ("guaranteed returns")
  • Dara ke bechna
